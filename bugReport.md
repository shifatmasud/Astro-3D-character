## Warning (Unexpected Behavior)

-   **[2026-05-22] (Resolved)**: Fixed weapon animation freezing on rapid clicks/attacks. GSAP's `killTweensOf` only terminates active running tweens, leaving subsequent queued `.to()` tweens scheduled later in a timeline to still execute and freeze the hand positions. Storing timelines in `leftTimelineRef` & `rightTimelineRef` and calling `.kill()` directly on them with a full reset (`gsap.set`) on new taps eliminates timeline overlap, while blocking keyboard auto-repeat ensures steady animations.
-   **[2026-05-22] (Resolved)**: Fixed slap animation striking at the butt/bottom level instead of cheek level. The hand pivot group's resting rotation has a `Math.PI` (180 degrees) rotation around Z, causing its local Y-axis coordinate system to be upside down (where positive `y` values push the hand downwards, and negative `y` values lift it upwards). Flipping the GSAP timeline's relative Y offsets from positive to negative (`y: -0.68` for wind-up and `y: -0.62` for contact) correctly pulls the hand upwards, landing a satisfying, high-fidelity slap directly at face/cheek level.
-   **[2026-05-22] (Resolved)**: Replaced limited custom mathematical coordinate-integration physics with robust, full-featured `@react-three/rapier` rigid body dynamic simulation.
